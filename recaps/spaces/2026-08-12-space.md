## The five things that mattered

**1. GodclouD vs Hurricane is set for August 23, 7pm EST.**
The number one artist challenges the founder. Hurricane is dropping six new songs
that night, his first releases of 2026. Candy made the flyer, which is pinned.

> "What happens when the number one artist challenges the founder. GodclouD versus
> Hurricane. One owns the top song, the other owns the battlefield." - 00:10:07

**2. The public API got pitched to the room, and it is already being used by agents.**
A reminder went out that `wavewarz.info` has an open stats API - no key, no signup,
point an agent at it and it pulls. Kwan has been using it on Quick Battle nights to
pull stats and turn them into content in real time.

> "There is an API for Wave Wars stats. Go to wavewarz.info, go to the API page, and
> just give it to your agent. It's an open public API. You don't need the API key." - 02:01:23

> "His agent said that it's very agent friendly." - 02:03:00

Ignite Radio asked for the link during the Space to put live WaveWarZ stats on their
own pages. The idea raised: showing battle stats next to a song so a listener can see
that a track is actually doing something, and who backed it first.

**3. Zabal Games finals now runs through WaveWarZ battles.**
15 people submitted during July's open submission. That gets cut to a top two in each
of three tracks - artists, builders, creators - announced at the end of this week. The
six finalists then get two weeks, to the end of August, to prepare. Each track settles
in a WaveWarZ battle: an artist battle, a builder battle, a creator battle.

Mentors are set: Onaji for creators, Zaal for builders, Hurricane for artists.
Judges are still open - that is an active ask to the community.

> "Top two artists, top two builders, and top two creators." - 00:50:53

**4. Ignite Radio and WaveWarZ are wired together.**
Ignite Radio is embedded at the bottom of `wavewarz.info`, so people reading the stats
can listen while they read. Ignite is building a portal so a track uploaded there can
be pushed onward without re-uploading. Their studio side now includes an automated
15-second promo clip generator and CSV export of plays, likes and shares.

**5. Fat Music proposed an integration. Nothing is agreed.**
Fat Music's founder proposed putting their web2 battle room into WaveWarZ as an
embedded, co-branded page, and explicitly declined to compete on battles.

> "I'm not going to play in your lane. The battle room that we have, I have it there,
> but I am not using it, and I'm not using it on purpose, because I want WaveWarZ to
> use it. You guys do it better than I could do it anyway." - 00:32:38

Stated plainly in the same breath: "We haven't decided anything. It's all just an idea
for now." Treat this as an open conversation, not a deal.
