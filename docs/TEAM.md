## Two names worth getting right

- **Stu Kwan (@stukwan) and Quan (@_0xQuan) are two different people.** Machine
  transcription frequently renders both as "Kwan" or "Quan"; they are distinct.
- **DJ Wavy is an AI judge, not a person.** It is one of the three signals that decide a
  Quick Battle (poll + DJ Wavy + SOL pool, best two of three). Its written reasoning is
  machine-generated - it refers to "Track A" and "Track B", never artist names - so do
  not attribute it to anyone.

## The ZAO

The wider community WaveWarZ sits inside is **The ZAO**, a decentralized artist
organization. Artists who battle earn on-chain, and ZAO members govern platform direction
through weekly Fractal sessions. WaveWarZ is The ZAO's flagship application.
