## Fan-made content

### Clippers program — structured community content creation

**Source:** https://wavewarz.info/ — VERIFIED (direct page fetch)

WaveWarZ runs a formal "Clippers" program: community members submit battle highlight clips for distribution across YouTube, X, and TikTok and receive point rewards. Submission happens via Telegram. This is the primary form of community-generated content — structured and incentivized rather than organic grassroots.

**Source:** https://x.com/Sir_Cut_Em_Up/status/1965675720488910872 — UNVERIFIED (HTTP 402, confirmed via search snippet)

**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rx0PeGv8lPI — UNVERIFIED (YouTube footer only; title from search snippet)

## Summary

- **Fan content is incentivized, not yet organic at scale.** The Clippers program produces community clips but it is rewards-driven. One organic external clipper was found (@Where4ArtThouRomeo). No fan Discord, fan X accounts, or meme culture found.
- **The roster is actively expanding beyond the top-48.** frameworkfortune and InkSpireMusic represent artists not previously in the known top-48 who appeared in battles and events in 2025–2026. Charity battle and Quick Battle format are the primary onboarding vectors.
- **Platform is running deliberate artist-spotlighting infrastructure.** YouTube Artist Interviews 2026 (XTinct Mar, Kata7yst Apr) plus Clippers rewards = structured community-building, not purely organic.
- **Two tournaments in recruitment phase.** 16-artist single-elim bracket + AI-artist bracket both actively recruiting — would significantly expand visible roster breadth when they launch.
