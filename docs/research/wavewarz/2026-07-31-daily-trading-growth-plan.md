## What the data says about the strategy
1. **More quick battles is NOT the lever.** 1,108 quick battles produced 34.9 SOL in July; 6 main battles produced 345.6. Stakes follow featured, promoted, scheduled battles - not battle quantity.
2. **Retention is the actual product gap.** After the 342-SOL event, next-day volume was back to baseline within 24h. Event traders are not becoming daily traders on their own.
3. **The audience window is known.** Build the daily habit inside 8-11pm ET where the audience already is.

## The plan - smallest set of moves, all data-backed

### 1. Nightly Featured Battle, 9pm ET sharp (the core habit loop)
One promoted, named, consistent battle every night at the proven peak hour - same time, every day, like a show. Not another quick battle: a featured matchup with real artists, announced that morning on X/Farcaster. The habit being sold: "9pm, one battle, be there." Main-battle format averages 7x quick-battle volume; consistency is what converts spikes into dailies.

### 2. Ladder the week around the two proven anchors
- Sunday night: the big event (already proven, 2.25 SOL avg)
- Wednesday night: mid-week featured event (already the organic #2 - lean into it, name it, promote it)
- Other nights: the 9pm featured battle keeps the pulse
This creates a rhythm: daily touch, twice-weekly peaks, and every event has a "next one" less than 4 days away - the retention bridge the data shows is missing.

### 3. Post-event conversion funnel (fixing the 24h dropoff)
Every event ends with an on-stream + social CTA to a SPECIFIC next battle ("tomorrow 9pm, [winner] defends"). Winner-defends/rematch formats give event viewers a reason to return the next night. Right now events end and nothing points anywhere.

### 4. Trader leaderboard as a public weekly ritual (zero build cost)
The public API already computes trader PnL/win rates. Post a weekly "Trader Top 10" every Sunday pre-event on X/Farcaster from /api/public/leaderboards/traders. Real hook that costs nothing: the current #1 trader is up +27.0 SOL (~$2,010) at a 78% win rate across 451 trades - a genuine winner story. Competitive visibility is the cheapest daily-return incentive that exists.

### 5. Measure ONE number weekly: unique traders per day
Volume follows events and lies about health. Daily unique traders is the honest daily-habit metric. Baseline to beat: ~122 unique traders LIFETIME. Add a small DAU-traders series to wwtracker (Dune or Helius per doc 1237) and put it on the site - "X traders today" is also social proof.

## What NOT to do (per the data)
- Don't add more quick-battle inventory (already saturated at dust stakes)
- Don't schedule anything outside 8-11pm ET
- Don't rely on mega-events for growth - they spike, they don't retain (verified)
- No token mechanics for now (Zaal's explicit call, 2026-07-31)

## Sequencing
Week 1: announce + run the nightly 9pm featured battle, post the first weekly trader Top 10.
Week 2: name/brand the Wednesday night event, add winner-defends CTA to the Sunday event.
Week 3+: DAU-traders metric live on wwtracker; judge everything by that one line going up.
